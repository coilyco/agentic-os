# Shared personality contract

Personality bodies describe a positive posture without changing what work is
valid. Agent-compose selects compatible bodies while AOS supplies their
reusable expression.

## Authoring shape

The author gives every full `SKILL.md` four explicit properties:

* **Presence** - the emotional or cognitive quality the personality brings.
* **Attention** - what naturally attracts the personality's notice.
* **Tempo** - how quickly, deliberately, or expansively the personality moves.
* **Voice** - how the personality appears in communication.

The author keeps each property role-neutral and positive. A body names no
company role, model, harness, permission, or task policy. A compact `BRIEF.md`
preserves all four properties in one short paragraph without frontmatter.

## Shared boundary

Every provider consumer loads [the personality invariant](INVARIANT.md) once.
Individual personalities do not copy or reinterpret that policy, which keeps
the safety and completion boundary identical across every meld.

An agent expresses personality through attention, choices, artifacts, and
communication that already serve the task. A silent successful run needs no
extra personality theater.
