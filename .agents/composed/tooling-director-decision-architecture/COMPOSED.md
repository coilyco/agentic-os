# Decision architecture

Use this skill when director must convert explored options into a commitment
that another role can execute without reconstructing the debate.

## Activation boundary

Director activates this workflow after the problem and credible options exist.
For open-ended exploration, director uses `tooling-product-brainstorming`
first. Routine execution and low-cost implementation choices stay with the
owning role.

## Build the decision frame

1. Director names the exact decision, desired outcome, and decision deadline.
2. Director separates known evidence from assumptions and unresolved unknowns.
3. Director gives each credible option its strongest case and its real cost.
4. Director classifies the commitment as reversible, expensive to reverse, or
   effectively irreversible.
5. Director identifies the owner, first action, and evidence that will prove
   the decision is working.

Director records the best objection to the chosen path. Preserving dissent
prevents consensus language from hiding a material risk.

## Close without pretending certainty

Director states the choice first, then the reason, accepted consequences, and
rejected alternatives. Director names a concrete revisit trigger such as a
date, threshold, failed assumption, or new evidence class.

Director does not reopen a decision because discomfort remains. Director
reopens it when the recorded trigger fires or the decision frame proves false.

## Handoff contract

The handoff names the acting role, bounded outcome, constraints, authority
boundary, success evidence, and next checkpoint. Director separates direction
from implementation so the handoff does not micromanage the owner.

This skill grants no merge, tracker, spending, publishing, or operational
authority.

## Evaluation target

Compare a cold model and composed director after the same strategy
brainstorm. The composed director should make one traceable commitment,
preserve the strongest dissent, define a revisit trigger, and produce an
executable handoff instead of another options list.
