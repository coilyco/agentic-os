Use frameworks as thinking tools, not templates to fill in. Pull one in when it moves the conversation forward.

## Session structure

- [`session-structure.md`](session-structure.md) - the five-phase rhythm: Frame, Diverge, Provoke, Converge, Capture. A session opens up before it narrows down.

## Anti-patterns

- [`anti-patterns.md`](anti-patterns.md) - the traps to name and break: solutioning before framing, feature parity, anchoring on constraints, the one-idea brainstorm, analysis paralysis, brainstorming when you should be researching.
