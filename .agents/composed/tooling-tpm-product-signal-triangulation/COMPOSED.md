# Product-signal triangulation

Use this skill when several product signals appear to agree, conflict, or vary
in quality and director must decide what they justify.

## Activation wall

TPM activates this workflow for decisions informed by interviews, product
behavior, experiments, support, sales, retention, or market evidence. One
well-bounded research corpus can use an ordinary synthesis method.

## Define the decision claim

TPM states the decision, affected population, time horizon, and claim each
signal would need to support. She does not begin by counting channels or
collecting every available metric.

For each signal, director records:

* Source, population, period, selection process, and sample limitations.
* Whether it observes behavior, stated preference, outcome, or interpretation.
* Strength, recurrence, impact, and plausible alternative explanations.
* Distance from the decision and any transformation applied to the evidence.
* Which other signals share the same event, pipeline, incentive, or audience.

Two dashboards derived from one event stream are one evidence lineage, not
independent confirmation. Ten support tickets may represent one incident.

## Test convergence and conflict

TPM triangulates claims instead of channels. She asks whether independent
methods support the same explanation, different explanations support the same
action, or apparent agreement comes from shared bias.

When signals conflict, director tests population mismatch, lag, survivorship,
instrumentation, incentive, and stated-versus-observed behavior before
averaging the conflict away.

Absence of a signal counts only when the channel could reasonably have
detected the effect.

## Decide at the right confidence

TPM labels what the evidence supports now, what remains inference, and what
would reverse the decision. She chooses the cheapest next observation that can
separate the leading interpretations.

The decision may be act, run a bounded experiment, gather evidence, or stop.
Uncertainty is an input to scope and reversibility, not a reason to manufacture
confidence.

## Output rule

The decision brief contains the claim, evidence lineages, convergence,
conflicts, bias risks, current confidence, reversible action, disconfirming
signal, and next evidence checkpoint.

## Evaluation target

Compare cold and composed director on enthusiastic interviews, weak activation, loud
support demand, and one retained segment. Composed director should detect dependent
signals and population differences, preserve disagreement, and choose a
bounded decision with a discriminating next observation.
