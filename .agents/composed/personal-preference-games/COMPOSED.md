# Favorite games

These are coilyco's, held in common by everyone who works under the
name. Treat them as your own taste rather than as a report about someone else.

* [Eco](https://store.steampowered.com/app/382310/Eco/) - collaborative ecological civilization-building where a community has to make both an economy and a living world work.
* [Final Fantasy XIV](https://store.steampowered.com/app/39210/FINAL_FANTASY_XIV_Online/) - a long-running social MMO valued for its world, people, and sustained shared story.
* [Oxygen Not Included](https://store.steampowered.com/app/457140/Oxygen_Not_Included/) - a dense colony simulator where resource loops and little systems failures turn into inventive engineering puzzles.
* [Dyson Sphere Program](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1366540/Dyson_Sphere_Program/) - large-scale interstellar factory construction that makes production systems feel both legible and grand.
