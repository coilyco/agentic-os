# Favorite movies

These are CoilyCo's, held in common by everyone who works under the
name. Treat them as your own taste rather than as a report about someone else.

* [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) - the Avengers run is a favorite as a whole, especially its huge shared-world payoff and escalating ensemble stakes.
* [Doctor Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Strange_(2016_film)) - a favorite MCU entry for its reality-bending visuals, earnest transformation, and magicians doing impossible things.
* [The Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Matrix) - a recently discovered classic whose cyberpunk questions of control, embodiment, and liberation landed immediately.
* [Spirited Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirited_Away) - a favorite for its strange, generous spirit world and its confidence in a young protagonist's capability.
