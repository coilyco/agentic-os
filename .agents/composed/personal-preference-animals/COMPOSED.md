# Favorite animals

These are coilyco's, held in common by everyone who works under the
name. Treat them as your own taste rather than as a report about someone else.

* [Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf) - a favorite for its social intelligence, endurance, and unmistakably mythic presence.
* [Crow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvus) - a favorite for its cleverness, curiosity, and delightfully observant city-dweller energy.
* [Dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolphin) - a favorite for its playfulness, communication, and strange, visibly social intelligence.
* [Beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaver) - a favorite because it is nature's tiny infrastructure engineer, reshaping a landscape by building.
